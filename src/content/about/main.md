I'm an AI engineer at **[Upstage](https://www.upstage.ai/)**. I build AI agents for document work.

Previously, I worked on automating evals for Solar, Upstage's foundation model. I also built a chat engine for SIU:, a member of [MAVE:](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mave:), a virtual K-pop idol group.

Before joining Upstage, I trained models at [Silvia Health](https://silvia.io/) to detect cognitive impairment from speech recorded on mobile devices.

During my master's degree, I [used active learning to make emotion recognition more data-efficient](https://github.com/Kaist-ICLab/AdaptiveESM). I also created [K-EmoCon](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3762961), a multimodal biosignal dataset for emotion recognition.

When I'm away from my laptop, I enjoy climbing, boxing, meditation, and time with my family.
